Malware and Cyber Threats

Historical Case: The 2024 Ticketmaster Data Breach

On May 20, 2024, cyberhackers gained unauthorized access to the personal information of customers. This included phone numbers, email addresses, credit card information, and much more. After further investigation, the parent company of Ticketmaster detected fraudulent activity in a third-party database. May 27 was when the company became aware of the hacker selling customers' data online for a profit--something that could have been avoided if they would have utilized a two factor authenticator or encouraging the use of strong passwords. Because of this, Ticketmaster had to confront their customers and inform them of this scandal. This data breach is significant because it further demonstrates the risks of using third-party platforms to store customer data, rather than their own. While this caused a lot of disruption for the company, everyone involved learned the importance of implementing prevention strategies, as this similar incident could happen to anyone.

Types of Malware

  1. Viruses: Software that is written to cause damage.
  2. Trojan Horses: Malware that is viewed as a normal software to trick users into installing it, enabling it to collect valuable information in a short period of time.

Common Hacker Motives

  1. Revenge: Battle between two or more companies to collect as much information that can be used against the company. Most times, this is because of personal conflicts.
  2. Personal Information Theft: Hackers steal data meant to be confidential and improperly use it or sell it to make a profit.

Prevention Strategies

  • Cybersecurity Training: Educating more people on the modern ways hackers are able to steal information and potential solutions will help more computer-users recognize malicious activity before it causes unsolvable conflict.
  • Multi-Factor Authentication: Additional step in the verification process to help limit unauthorized users. An example of this is Texas A&M University utilizing the Duo Mobile Platform to provide a unique passcode to Aggies when signing into the Howdy Portal.
